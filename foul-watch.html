    How it works

    Data. The page reads ESPN's public scoreboard and play-by-play straight from your browser. Games near the bonus refresh every 20 seconds, and the rest every 45. ESPN can trail the TV broadcast, so check the scorebug before you bet. Browsers slow down timers in background tabs, so keep this tab in front for the fastest updates.

    NBA. Team fouls reset every quarter. From the 5th team foul in a quarter (4th in overtime), every common foul is 2 free throws, so a team at 4 is at the limit. In the last 2:00, a team under the limit gets one foul to give. Offensive fouls, technicals and double fouls don't count.

    NCAAB. Team fouls reset at halftime; overtime continues the 2nd half. The 7th team foul starts the 1-and-1 and the 10th starts 2 shots, so a team at 6 is one away. Offensive fouls count toward the total but never shoot. Technicals aren't counted.

    Quarter and half totals. "Pace" is the points so far in the quarter (NBA) or half (NCAAB, overtime included) plus what the rest of it usually scores in games like this one, with neither team in the bonus. That takes in the time left, this game's scoring rate and pregame total, the score margin, how often fouls are being called, free-throw shooting, and players in foul trouble. "With the bonus" adds what each team being in the bonus added in 2,631 NBA and 4,453 college games from 2024-25 on. In the NBA, the bonus is worth more the longer it lasts before the last-2:00 foul to give: about 1.1 points with 3:00 left, 1.9 at 4:00 and 2.6 at 5:00. In a college 2nd half it's about 1.3 with 4:00 left and 2.4 with 8:00 left. In a 1st half it's about 1.1. On games it wasn't fitted on, the projection landed within a few tenths of a point of the result on average in every quarter, half, time left, score margin and foul state checked. Any single quarter still swings about 5 points either way, and a college half about 7, so treat these as a guide, not a fair line.

    Edge test. When you check the kiosk on an alert, tap Log kiosk line and enter the line you see, bet or not. Once the quarter or half ends, the page fills in the result from ESPN and keeps score: how far your side beat the line on average, against what the vig needs at your odds, with a 95% range. Logging the ones you pass on too keeps the test honest. Separately, every both-in-the-bonus alert is recorded and graded on its own, which checks whether the page's numbers still hold this season. Records stay in this browser only; Download CSV saves a copy.

    Player points. "On the floor" lists each team's players on the court with the most free-throw attempts so far. With both teams in the bonus, the top one scored about 0.13 points a minute more than usual for the rest of the quarter.
