    How it works

    Data. The page reads ESPN's public scoreboard and play-by-play straight from your browser. Games near the bonus refresh every 20 seconds, and the rest every 45. ESPN can trail the TV broadcast, so check the scorebug before you bet. Browsers slow down timers in background tabs, so keep this tab in front for the fastest updates.

    NBA. Team fouls reset every quarter. From the 5th team foul in a quarter (4th in overtime), every common foul is 2 free throws, so a team at 4 is at the limit. In the last 2:00, a team under the limit gets one foul to give. Offensive fouls, technicals and double fouls don't count.

    NCAAB. Team fouls reset at halftime; overtime continues the 2nd half. The 7th team foul starts the 1-and-1 and the 10th starts 2 shots, so a team at 6 is one away. Offensive fouls count toward the total but never shoot. Technicals aren't counted.

    Quarter and half totals. "Pace" is the points so far in the quarter (NBA) or half (NCAAB, overtime included) plus the time left at this game's scoring rate. "With the bonus" adds what both teams being in the bonus added in 449 NBA and 443 college games. That's about 0.41 points a minute in the NBA, 0.44 a minute at the college 1-and-1, and roughly 0.5 at the double bonus (few cases). The rest of a quarter swings about 5 points either way, so treat these as a rough guide, not a fair line.

    Player points. "On the floor" lists each team's players on the court with the most free-throw attempts so far. With both teams in the bonus, the top one scored about 0.13 points a minute more than usual for the rest of the quarter.
